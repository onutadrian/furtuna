Introduction

SanoPass came to me with a big idea and no roadmap: subscription that gives users access to a network of clinics.



I mapped their UX from scratch, delivered the MVP over a weekend, and kept building from there. Resulting in a product that evolved into an all-in-one platform for medical services, fitness, and nutrition, and helped land a €5M acquisition by MedLife, Romania’s largest private healthcare provider.

Making healthcare scheduling work like it should

Calling clinics, juggling availability, and messy booking flows?
I killed all of that.

Drag

SanoPass expanded quickly, from healthcare to fitness and beyond. I designed a goal-based fitness onboarding that matched users with the right plan in seconds. Show up, scan a QR code, and get moving.

At first, SanoPass just wanted a good-looking UI. What they got was a full UX partner. I mapped journeys, clarified the architecture, and collaborated with a dev team that didn’t even speak the same language; literally.

I supported the product until the acquisition, testing features, fixing edge cases, and keeping the experience tight.

I made it easy to find a gym, clinic, or service nearby with a clean, built-in map. Filter by location, availability, or service type, no digging, no confusion. Just tap and go.
