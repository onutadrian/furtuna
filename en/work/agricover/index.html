Introduction

Agricover brings together business lines, solutions, and advanced technologies in a structured portfolio that creates value for farmers, supports daily work in the field, and helps move Romanian agriculture forward.

Agricover needed a mobile product that simplifies the financial relationship between farmers and their business. The app gives quick access to credit, payments, and agricultural products, with the flexibility daily farm work requires.

Enrollment

I designed a clear onboarding flow that works for both existing and new customers.

The steps are grouped into easy-to-follow stages, each with its own visual identity, creating a simple, low-effort experience.

Wallet

The wallet brings accounts, balances, and day-to-day operations into one place.

Farmers can top up the account, initiate payments, exchange currency, and track transactions while keeping funds and available credit in view.

Full feature set

The app gives farmers a complete financial workspace where cards, accounts, payments, credit, and transactions live in one place.

From managing cards and starting payments to accessing credit and reviewing transactions, the critical actions stay close at hand. One coherent experience built for the real pace of farm work.

Farmers exchange

Bursa Fermierilor gives fast access to market information relevant to farm operations.

In one place, users can follow grain, fuel, and fertilizer prices both locally and on the exchange, making better-informed commercial decisions.

Credit lines

Credit becomes easier to understand and manage. Users can see due amounts, current and upcoming deadlines, and the remaining balance for each credit line at a glance.

I organized the information into a clear hierarchy that puts due dates and payment amounts first. The compact interface keeps the necessary context visible and naturally guides users toward important actions.

Drag

Invoices

Invoices are centralized for review and payment directly in the app, in full or in part depending on the farm's needs. In the same space, users can keep track of documents and eventually sign credit contracts without extra trips.

Signing a financing offer or credit contract becomes simple and fast. Users can review the documents, confirm their agreement, and complete the process directly in the app without extra trips or unnecessary steps.
