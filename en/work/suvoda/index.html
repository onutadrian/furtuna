Beinging patients a sense of calm and control

A single, intuitive app where patients can schedule visits, submit receipts and receive payments, answer questionnaires, view travel details, and receive reminders.

Suvoda reached out help them build a patient payments app. Which, at the time, nobody had. The app evolved into a complete solution for patients with payments, questionnaires, travel, scheduling and more.

Real-time, local currency payments through the patient’s preferred method.

Suvoda’s patient app is designed with real people in mind, using user-centered design principles and the latest in consumer technology to create a streamlined, intuitive experience. Every interaction is thoughtfully designed to support patients, helping them stay engaged throughout their trial experience. 

It’s not about lectures or guilt trips. It’s about meeting people where they are and giving them the tools to make smarter financial decisions every day.

Easy, Elegant, Flexible.

Every interaction is thoughtfully designed to support patients through a well designed visual system.

The Suvoda purple inspires a calm and hopeful feeling while enabling a modern chromatic through it’s product line.
